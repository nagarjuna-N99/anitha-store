Saree Care Guide

Preserve the beauty and longevity of your handloom treasures

Pure Silk Care (Kanjeevaram & Banarasi)

  • 🫧

    Washing

    Dry clean only for heavily zari-worked sarees. For plain silks, use mild shampoo in cold water. Do not soak for more than 5 minutes.

  • 💧

    Drying

    Never wring or twist. Dry in shade, away from direct sunlight to prevent color fading.

  • 🔥

    Ironing

    Iron on the reverse side using low to medium heat. Place a cotton cloth between the iron and saree.

  • ✨

    Special Note

    Silk naturally develops a soft sheen over time. Avoid contact with perfumes directly on the fabric.

Tussar & Raw Silk Care

  • 🫧

    Washing

    Hand wash gently with mild detergent in cold water. Tussar is delicate; avoid vigorous rubbing.

  • 💧

    Drying

    Roll in a clean towel to absorb excess water. Dry flat in shade. Never hang wet Tussar.

  • 🌡️

    Ironing

    Use low heat. Iron while slightly damp for best results. Always iron on the reverse side.

  • 🌿

    Natural Texture

    Tussar has a natural coarse texture and slubs. This is not a defect but a mark of authentic handloom.

Cotton & Khadi Care

  • 🧺

    Washing

    Machine wash on gentle cycle or hand wash. Khadi may shrink slightly on first wash.

  • ☀️

    Drying

    Sun drying is acceptable for white cottons. For colored cottons, dry in shade.

  • 🔥

    Ironing

    Cotton can withstand high heat. Iron while damp for crisp finish.

  • 🌀

    Wrinkles

    Khadi and handloom cottons wrinkle easily. This is a sign of pure natural fiber.

Long-term Storage

  • 📦

    Folding

    Fold sarees along original lines. Refold every 3-4 months to prevent permanent crease damage.

  • 🌿

    Natural Repellents

    Use dried neem leaves, lavender sachets, or cedar blocks. Avoid naphthalene balls.

  • 🌬️

    Breathing

    Store in muslin cloth bags, never plastic. Plastic traps moisture and causes yellowing.

  • ☀️

    Airing

    Take sarees out once a season. Air them in shade for a few hours to keep fibers fresh.
