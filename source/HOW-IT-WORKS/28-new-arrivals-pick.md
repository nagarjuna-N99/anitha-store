# 28 · Choosing the New Arrivals sarees (4 Nov 2026)

Home Page → Other home page sections → **New Arrivals slider**. Under the heading boxes there is now a list of **all the sarees already on your website**, grouped by category. Tick the ones you want in the slider. Only published sarees are listed (not saved drafts).

- Nothing ticked: the slider shows every saree marked new, as before.
- Something ticked: only those show, in the order you ticked them.
- **Clear my choice** goes back to "all new".
- A ticked saree you later delete just disappears from the slider.
The Signature collection tiles already open one of your categories, chosen from your category list.
