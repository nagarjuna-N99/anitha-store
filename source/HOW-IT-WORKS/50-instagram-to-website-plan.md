## 0. A word about "Hold" (answering "hold for what?")
Because every design is one piece, a customer who says "I want it" needs a little time to pay. **Hold** = the owner presses one button and that piece is kept for **that customer only** for a fixed time (suggested 24 hours), shown to everyone else as "being confirmed". It stops two people paying for the same piece. It is optional and never automatic. When the time ends the piece becomes available again and the owner is told.

## 1. The idea in one line
**Instagram is the shop window; the website is the counter, the memory and the proof.**
Instagram brings attention. The website turns it into things Instagram cannot give: a page per design that Google can find, a list of customers who agreed to hear from the shop (so you do not depend on one platform), the order approval and tracking flow already built, and answers to "price?" without a conversation. **Do not stop DMs**: many buyers like to message. The route is: they see a piece, they get the link, they either order on the page or message from it with the design already named.

## 4. The weekly routine (the part that makes it work)
**A weekly "drop"**: because every piece is single, new pieces arriving on a fixed day is natural scarcity that is true. Example: every week on one evening, 5 to 8 new pieces. Order of release: **website first (a few hours early), then reels and stories**. Followers learn that the website has it first.
**Content per week (suggested minimum)**: 3 reels (a close-up of the pallu or border, how it is worn, packing and dispatch), daily stories (new piece, "only 1 piece left", client photo with permission, behind the scenes), 2 carousels (styling one piece 3 ways, fabric explained). **Every reel and story ends with one action**: "Details and price: link in bio" or a **link sticker** to that design's page (stories can carry a link sticker; captions cannot be tapped, so say "link in bio" there).
**One honest rule**: never promise views, results or discounts that are not real; say "only 1 piece" only when it is true (the website takes the piece off when sold, so the two always agree).

## 5. How people are routed (the three roads)
| Where they are | What they do | Where they land |
|---|---|---|
| Reel, post | tap the name in the bio, then the link | the website, "New this week" |
| Story | tap the link sticker | that design's page |
| DM or comment "price" | assistant answers, then asks "shall I send the page?" (only after yes) | that design's page, or WhatsApp to order |
| WhatsApp (old customers, forwarded reels) | assistant answers from the stock | the page link after yes (guide 48) |
Customers who prefer to buy by chat can: the page has the WhatsApp button naming the design; the owner approves the order as already built.

## 6. Collect your own audience (so Instagram is not the only door)
- On the website: "Tell me about new pieces" (WhatsApp opt-in, built as "similar sarees" alerts) and the email notes (built). One line on every design page: "Get the next drop first".
- On Instagram: story question "Want a reminder before the next drop? Message DROP" so the customer starts the chat (that starts the 24-hour window and counts as opt-in); the owner or assistant adds them to the drop list.
- Never message anyone who did not ask; STOP always works (built).

## 7. What to measure (weekly, 10 minutes)
Instagram: profile visits, link taps, reach of the 3 best reels. Website (our own simple counter, source tags `src=ig-bio`, `ig-story`, `ig-reel`, `wa`): visitors by source, design pages opened, WhatsApp button taps, orders. Shop: orders from Instagram DM vs website vs WhatsApp, repeat customers, sold within 24 hours. **Decision rule**: after 8 weeks, if the website brings more orders per visitor than DMs do, make "price: link in bio" the default answer; if not, fix the pages (photos, price shown, trust) before spending money on ads. **No paid ads until the organic route works for a month**; then start small (a link to the "New this week" page, or click-to-WhatsApp), with a small daily limit and one design at a time.
