# 49 · Browsing the shop: cards, search, filters, Quick View, the saree page, hover effects, Follow us (7 Nov 2026)

Code: `src/js/428-browse-upgrade.js`, `src/css/200-browse-upgrade.css`, `productCardHTML` / `cardGo` in `src/js/070-storefront-logic.js`, `initShopToolbar` and the search bar in `src/js/190-shop-search-filters-sort.js`, the page bar in `src/html/050-admin-view.html`. Test: `tests/browse-test.cjs`.

## What a customer sees
- **The card is one big link.** Pressing anywhere on it (photo, name, price) opens the saree. On a computer, moving the mouse over it shows **another photo of the same saree** (when the saree has two or more) and round buttons appear on the photo: **bag**, **Quick View** (the eye), **share**, and the heart. On a phone the bag and eye are always shown (share is on the saree page). A sold-out saree shows a bell ("tell me when it is back") instead of the bag. The name gets an underline while the mouse is over the card. Most sarees are a single piece, so the badge says "One of a kind".
- **Quick View**: a small window with the photos, name, price, "only 1 available", short text, **Add to bag**, **View full details** and Wishlist. Esc, the × or a tap outside closes it. On a phone it is a sheet from the bottom.
- **A saree opens as a real page**, not a pop-up: it fills the screen with a bar at the top (**← Back to the collection**, the saree name, **Bag**), the whole page scrolls once, photos and details sit side by side and "Similar sarees" stay in a column on the right (under the photos on a phone). The address is still `/product/<name>/`, so the browser's back button and shared links work.
- **Search bar**: the 🔍 opens a wide, rounded search bar. As you type, the **matching sarees appear right under the bar** (photo, name with the matching letters highlighted, category, price, "sold out" / "single piece"), up to 6, with "See all N results". Arrow keys move, Enter opens the highlighted saree (or all results when none is highlighted), Esc closes. With nothing typed it shows the categories as buttons. A word that matches nothing says so and offers the categories. The Collection page is only changed when the customer presses Enter or "See all".
- **Filter bar** (like a big saree shop): **Show Filters** (opens the full panel; the button then says Hide Filters), **Price / Colour / Fabric** drop-downs (a mark shows when one is in use; they close when you press elsewhere or Esc), **N ITEMS**, **Sort**. 4 cards across on a computer, 3 on a tablet, 2 on a phone.
- **Photos that cannot load** show a calm "Photo coming soon" card instead of broken text. A second hover photo that cannot load is simply not used.
- **Hover effects**: menu and footer links, category names and "Moment" tiles get an underline that grows; tiles and cards lift; the photo of a **category tile changes while the mouse is over it** (it shows other sarees of that category) and goes back afterwards; Find-it-faster tiles tilt their icon. Nothing moves for people who ask their phone for reduced motion.
- **Follow us** at the bottom of every page: Facebook (https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100086623513019) and Instagram (`anitha_pemmasani`, from the shop's Instagram page). Only Facebook / Instagram addresses are accepted; anything else is never shown.

## What the owner / admin can do
- **Admin → Home Page → Follow us**: the Facebook link, the Instagram name or link (for example `anithapemmasani` or the full link) and a switch. The Instagram name starts as `anitha_pemmasani`; change it here if it ever changes. An empty box hides that button.
- **Owner → Products** shows a card when starter **sample sarees** (the app's demo sarees with stock photos) are still in the shop, with a button **Remove the N sample sarees**. These samples are **never sent to customers** and the WhatsApp assistant never offers them (server: `isSampleProduct` in `wa-assistant/src/shopdata.mjs`: an id like `p1` to `p99` whose photos are all stock-photo links). Your own sarees are never touched. Until real sarees are added, the public shop is empty.
- `SHOW_SAMPLES=1` (set only by `run-tests.sh` for the browser suites that run on demo data) makes the server show the samples again. Never set it on the live server.

## Why "old photos still showing" happened
The live shop still held the app's **starter demo sarees** (stock photos from a photo website). Some of those links no longer load (broken pictures with alt text such as "Ivory Gold Paithani"), and the others are demo pictures, not your sarees. The earlier fix made sure no **old copy** of the shop is shown; this fix removes the **demo content itself** from what customers get, and a picture that fails shows "Photo coming soon". To fill the shop: Owner → Image Studio / Products → add your real sarees with your own photos (each saree can have several photos; the second one is the hover photo).
